Origin of Sonny Angels

Sonny Angels are collectible figurines that originated in Japan. They are known for their cute and whimsical designs, often featuring various themes and costumes. Each figure is typically packaged in a blind box, adding an element of surprise to the collecting experience. Sonny Angels have gained popularity worldwide among collectors and enthusiasts of cute and unique toys.
